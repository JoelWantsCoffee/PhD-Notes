Suppose $A$ is augmented algebra over $R$ with counit $\epsilon : A \to R$. A (left) fox derivative is a map
$$
\begin{align*}
\partial &: A \to A\\
\partial (ab) &= \epsilon(a)\partial(b) + \partial(a)b
\end{align*}
$$
Consider the following example of a left fox derivative.
$$
\begin{align*}
\partial : \mathbb{C}[G*F(z)] &\to \mathbb{C}[G * F(z)]\\
\partial(g) = 0 &\quad\partial(z) = 1
\end{align*}
$$
