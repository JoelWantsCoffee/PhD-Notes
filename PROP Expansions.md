Recall that an (additive) ProP ("_Products and Permutation Category_") is an (additive) strict symmetric monoidal category whose objects are the naturals, and whose monoidal product is addition. The algebra category of an (additive) Prop $T$ over a (additive) symmetric monoidal category $\mathscr{C}$ is the category of (additive) symmetric monoidal functors and symmetric monoidal nat. transforms.
$$ \mathscr{C}^T=\mathrm{Alg}(T,\mathscr{C}) := [T,\,\mathscr{C}]_\mathrm{symmetric\ monoidal}
$$
It follows that the assignment $(-)^T : \mathsf{SMonCat} \to \mathsf{Cat}$ is functorial.

***example.*** The additive prop for Lie bialgebras $\mathrm{LieBi}$ is generated by $\mu : 2 \to 1$ and $\delta : 1 \to 2$, subjected to the obvious relations.

Now, recall the associated graded functor $\operatorname{gr} : \mathsf{Fil}\mathscr{A} \to \mathsf{Fil}\mathscr{A}$ is symmetric monoidal for symmetric monoidal abelian $\mathscr{A}$. It follows there are idempotent semicomonads $\mathrm{gr}^T : \mathsf{Fil}\mathscr{A}^T \to \mathsf{Fil}\mathscr{A}^T$ for all props $T$. Moreover, recall that $\mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr})$ inherits a symmetric monoidal structure from $\mathsf{Fil}\mathscr{A}$.

***lemma.*** Writing $\mathrm{Drin}$ for the category of Drinfel'd associators, there is a functor $\mathrm{Drin} \to \mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr}^\mathrm{LieBi})$.

***lemma.*** Writing $\mathrm{Tang}$ for the tangles prop, there is a functor $\mathrm{Drin} \to \mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr}^\mathrm{Tang})$.

***lemma.*** $\mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr}^T) \cong \mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr})^T$

***lemma.*** Suppose $\varphi : T \to S$ is a morphism of props (i.e. a strict symmetric monoidal functor). It follows there is a functor $\phi : \mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr}^T) \to \mathrm{hexp}(\mathrm{gr}^S)$.
